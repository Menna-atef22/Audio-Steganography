ℹ️ Understanding DWT Parameters

Wavelet Family

Different wavelets offer different time-frequency properties. Daubechies (db) wavelets are commonly used for audio steganography.

Decomposition Level

Higher levels provide deeper frequency decomposition but require longer audio files. Level 2-3 is typically optimal.

Embedding Strength

Controls message amplitude. Higher values increase robustness but may introduce audible artifacts at extreme values.

Target Bandwidth

Specifies frequency range for embedding. Lower frequencies are less noticeable but less robust; higher frequencies are more robust.
