ℹ️ Decoding Tips & Troubleshooting

Parameter Matching

The wavelet family and decomposition level must exactly match the parameters used during encoding.

Sensitivity Tuning

If decoding fails, try adjusting extraction sensitivity. Lower sensitivity is more lenient but may introduce errors; higher sensitivity is stricter.

Error Correction

Enable error correction if the message is slightly corrupted or if the audio has been compressed or modified.

Audio Quality

Ensure the audio file is in the same format and quality as the original encoded file. Conversions or compressions may prevent recovery.
