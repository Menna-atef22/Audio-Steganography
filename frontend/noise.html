ℹ️ About Noise Testing & Metrics

Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)

Measured in decibels (dB). Higher values indicate cleaner signals. 20 dB = moderate noise, 40 dB = very clean.

Bit Error Rate (BER)

Percentage of message bits corrupted by noise. Lower is better. 0% = perfect recovery, >10% = significant corruption.

Success Rate

Overall message recovery accuracy. Accounts for both bit errors and overall coherence of extracted message.

Noise Types

  • Gaussian Noise: Random white noise with normal distribution (realistic background noise)
  • White Noise: Uniform noise across all frequencies (synthetic test condition)
